Privacy policy

How this website handles enquiry and optional analytics data.

Last reviewed: July 2026

Information you provide

When you contact ALZA, you may provide your name, work email, company, role, migration stage, and message. This information is used to respond to your enquiry and manage the resulting business conversation.

Analytics and tracking

Analytics and advertising tags are not configured in this build. If production IDs are added, optional tags are designed to load only after consent unless the configuration is deliberately changed. Consent choices are stored in the browser.

Traffic attribution

The site stores basic first-touch campaign parameters and referring-page information in the browser to preserve enquiry context. This can be removed by clearing site data.

Third-party services

Configured form, chatbot, analytics, CRM, and automation providers process information under their own terms. ALZA should update this policy with the selected providers before activation.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@alzatotaltech.com.
