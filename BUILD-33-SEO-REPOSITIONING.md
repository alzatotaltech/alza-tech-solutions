# Build 33 Entity & SEO Repositioning

This build repositions ALZA Total Tech Solutions FZ-LLC as a UAE IT consulting and complete technology solutions company while retaining ALZA M365 Migration Suite as the flagship proprietary software product.

Implemented:
- Consistent company descriptor: IT Infrastructure, Cloud, Cybersecurity and Software.
- Visible home and office IT setup, networks, servers, storage, backup, cloud, security and managed-support services.
- Strong flagship product positioning for ALZA M365 Migration Suite.
- Unique titles and meta descriptions for primary pages.
- Organization / ProfessionalService, WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList and SoftwareApplication JSON-LD.
- Stable site name, favicon, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter metadata.
- Updated sitemap priorities and last-modified dates.
- Broad enquiry language across contact, modal and WhatsApp links.

Important: no technical change can guarantee first position in Google. Search visibility depends on indexing, query intent, competition, authority, links, reviews, content quality and time.
