Use cases

Different business events. The same need for control.

Migration programs change shape depending on the transaction, organization, and delivery model. The planning system should adapt without losing governance.

Mergers & acquisitions

Bring acquired users into a target Microsoft 365 environment.

Coordinate discovery, identity decisions, workload waves, business owners, communications, and handover.

Divestitures

Separate data and collaboration under hard boundaries.

Make scope, dependencies, access decisions, exceptions, and completion evidence visible.

Tenant consolidation

Reduce fragmentation across Microsoft 365 estates.

Standardize planning and decisions while respecting different workload and business contexts.

Modernization

Move from legacy operating patterns to a governed cloud model.

Connect migration activity with information architecture, ownership, adoption, and support readiness.

Managed service providers

Create a repeatable migration delivery framework.

Internal IT teams
