License boundaries

Capacity that is clear before purchase.

The revised plans use defined seat, session, concurrency, and storage allowances so the operating entitlement is clear before purchase.

Licensed seats

The maximum number of mailbox users, users, or SharePoint sites covered by the selected subscription.

Registered sessions

The number of active administrator sessions permitted under the selected tier.

Concurrent jobs

The maximum number of migration jobs that may execute simultaneously under the subscription.

Licensed storage

The shared or total migration storage pool included with the selected plan. Enterprise tiers marked Unlimited* remain subject to the written agreement and technical limits.

*Unlimited Enterprise storage

Unlimited* means ALZA does not publish a fixed storage-pool ceiling for that Enterprise tier. Microsoft service quotas and throttling, supported workloads, available customer infrastructure, network capacity, acceptable-use terms, and the executed order still apply.
