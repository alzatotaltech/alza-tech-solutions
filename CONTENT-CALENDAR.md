## Production workflow

For each article:

1. Confirm one buyer question and one primary keyword.
2. Interview an ALZA subject-matter expert.
3. Add first-hand examples without identifying a customer unless approved.
4. Link to one product/service page and one related resource.
5. Add Article and Breadcrumb schema.
6. Review technical accuracy, claims, privacy, and trademarks.
7. Publish, submit the URL for indexing, and review qualified traffic after
   30–60 days.

Avoid generic AI-written volume. One useful, reviewed article is more valuable
than several thin pages.
